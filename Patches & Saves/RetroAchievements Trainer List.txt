Other fun "Easter Eggs" / Achievement Ideas:

- "High Roller" - Catch Voltorb, Electrode, Komala, and Spheal (explanation: those 4 Pokémon have follower sprites that roll instead of walk!)

- "Face-to-Face" - Battle "Pkmn Trainer 1 Nemo" (me!) at Wakewater Isle

- "Lazarus Taxa" - Register all Fossil Pokémon plus Relicanth in the Pokédex

- "Pinball Prodigy" - Collect 10 Pinball Points at once (earn 1 per Pinball game won)

- "Man, I Love Fish" - Get the Old Rod, Good Rod, and Super Rod

- "One-Way Trip" - Obtain a Strange Souvenir

- Plus all the other obvious stuff like completing the Pokédex, getting all TMs, etc!
